## Design Context

### Users
Private experimental design sandbox. The creator is the sole audience — a designer/developer
exploring what's possible. No usability constraints from external stakeholders. Ambition is the
primary metric.

### Brand Personality
"ceremonial, unhurried, precise"

### Aesthetic Direction
Editorial & still — a page from a Michelin three-star tasting menu booklet. Warm cream paper,
considered white space, authoritative serif headlines, restrained sans-serif body text. Light
theme, contrasting deliberately against the dark Seoul Night Bites section immediately above.
No glassmorphism, no glows, no card boxes. The silence between elements is part of the design.

### Design Principles
1. White space is not empty — it carries weight and ceremony.
2. One accent color, used sparingly. Warmth over flash.
3. Typography does the heavy lifting. Decoration is suspect.
4. Every layout decision should feel like it was made by a typographer at a French publisher.
5. The "unforgettable" element: large ghost course numbers floating behind the text — depth
   through layering, not effects.
